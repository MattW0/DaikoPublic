> [!info] Description
> - Items originally made of cake, that became real once they left the banishment realm of [[History/Beings/Gabriel|Gabriel]]

**Fight fire with fire:**
Metal pick for playing hardcore music. Can cast firebolt as an cantrip or use its daily energy to cast Scorching rays (lvl 2) once. After this usage the Pick has no more charge until a long rest has been completed.

**Hairlock:**
Strand of a Genies hair that can store an unused spell slot for 24 hours.

**Royal Gland:**
Spray a glob of royal jelly to summon an swarm of ants for 1 minute once per day.

**Mimictorynox:**
Mimic cursed to stay in the form of an all purpose tool. You have to feed it and take care of it. It can transform into a variety of tools; as an action, you can touch the item and transform it into any type of artisan's tool of your choice. Whatever form the tool takes, you are proficient with it. While holding this tool, you gain a +1 bonus to the spell attack rolls and the saving throw DCs of your artificer spells.

**Galapachocker:**
The necklace imbues the wearers wildshape with additional protection. Twice per shortrest and while in wildshape the wearer can raise its AC by 1d6 with a reaction by projecting a spectral Turtleshell.
