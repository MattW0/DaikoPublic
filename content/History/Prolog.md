800 years ago, on a continent called [[Daiko|Daiko]], the clever and craving humankind, discovered something dark and radiant deep beneath the earth's crust: [[History/Lore/Obsidian|Obsidian]]. Drawn by its arcane potential, they learned to process it, unlocking unprecedented access to the [[History/Lore/Weave|Weave]]. The strong became mages. The weak became their soldiers. And tribes formed into small kingdoms. Thus began the **[[History/Ages/Age of Dominion|Age of Dominion]]**.

The humans claimed the godless magic for themselves and spread like wind-blown fire across the land, exploring lands far west and north. In their hunger for control, they traded and clashed with the northern powers of the elves in [[Nations/The Freelands/The Freelands|The Freelands]] and the dwarven clans of the [[Nations/Ironreach/Ironreach|Ironreach]], unsettling the balance of the world. Other ancient magical beings—fey, elemental, celestial—were captured and exploited for their abilities, those who could fled into the far north west and watched the rise of Obsidian with wary eyes.

However, the human kingdoms, wielded, consumed, and hoarded [[History/Lore/Obsidian|Obsidian]] in excess. It was their fuel for conquest and sorcery, but it drank from the soul and consumed them. Many of them fell to its dark temptations or waged wars amongst themselves. Only the strongest kingdoms, with abundant sources of the arcane material prevailed. From that dark history and continuous war, colonization and exploitation, humans had to accept that "Those who use [[History/Lore/Obsidian|Obsidian]] for too long are changed, first in thought, then in flesh and always, in allegiance."

Entering the [[History/Ages/Age of Enlightenment|Age of Enlightenment]] 300 years ago, the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Daicon Kingdom|Daicon Kingdom]] was the first to discover, that [[History/Lore/Obsidian|Obsidian]]'s arcane energy could animate material itself. The result was a surge of innovation—engines, weapons, or more recently, [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Lore/Magitech|Magitech]] automatons, seeped into the daily lives and took over nearly every laboring task. With less need for manual forced labor and foreign products, colonizing efforts became less attractive. 

With their focus for development turned inward, society prospered and other kingdoms feared the power the Daicons achieved. They scrambled to imitate their designs, yet without Daiconian refinement methods, and without control over the inner corruption, their attempts often ended in ruin. One by one, the human nations collapsed and only three endured. Amongst them the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Daicon Kingdom|Daicon Kingdom]], disciplined, ordered, and rich from their dominance in arcane industry. Only [[Nations/Atheria - Solar Federation/Atheria - Solar Federation|Atheria - Solar Federation]] managed to turn away from Obsidian and find peace in a simpler life.

It was in the closing decades of the [[History/Ages/Age of Enlightenment|Age of Enlightenment]] that the Daicons began to tighten their grip on the one thing they still feared: magic itself. Any kind of extraction, trade and use of [[History/Lore/Obsidian|Obsidian]] became highly controlled and regulated. The largest industrial conglomerate, publicly known as [[Nations/The Gray Ring/The Grey Ring|The Grey Ring]], established its economic power as a monopoly with close ties to the monarchy and its council. In a joint effort to reduce chaos, they formed the [[Nations/The Gray Ring/Lore/MICE|MICE]] agency, which marks the beginning of the [[History/Ages/Age of Shadows|Age of Shadows]]. 

MICE as in "Magic & Intelligence Control Enforcement" grew from a regulatory body to a militant agency. With their authoritarian status the agents can move and act freely in the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Daicon Kingdom|Daicon Kingdom]]. Their job is to punish the use of magic in any form and to protect and further the goals of a uniform and disciplined society. Even the former Queen, [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/NPCs/Elen the Radiant|Elen the Radiant]], who was more a symbol than sovereign, had little sway over [[Nations/The Gray Ring/Lore/MICE|MICE]]’s true operations. 

Soon, rumors about a resistance group known as the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Redfen/Lore/Maquis|Maquis]] lead to a crack down on any kind of rebellion. Because the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Towns/Varenhold/Iron Aegis|Iron Aegis]], the royal guard, refused to coorperate with [[Nations/The Gray Ring/Lore/MICE|MICE]], the monarchy was overturned, the queen beheaded and state affairs are now soley decided by the council. From the war on [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Towns/Varenhold/Varenhold|Varenhold]] and various tragic incidents such as in the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Redfen/Redfen|Redfen]], many fugitives from the Daicon Kingdom and neighboring regions seek refuge in other parts of the continent... 

This marks the present day on Daiko, 22. July 30 AS.
