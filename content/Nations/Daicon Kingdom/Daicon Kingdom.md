Nowadays the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/NPCs/Council|Council]] has authoritarian power and enforces rules through [[Nations/The Gray Ring/Lore/MICE|MICE]]. 

The queen [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/NPCs/Elen the Radiant|Elen the Radiant]] ruled as a monarch over the capital [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Towns/Varenhold/Varenhold|Varenhold]] until 26 AS. Various resistance group fought the takeover with guerilla tactics, but were eventually quenched by the overwhelming [[Nations/The Gray Ring/Lore/MICE|MICE]] forces.

The monarchy had the following powers: 
* Private elite guard: [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Towns/Varenhold/Iron Aegis|Iron Aegis]]
* Can veto the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/NPCs/Council|Council]]'s decisions
