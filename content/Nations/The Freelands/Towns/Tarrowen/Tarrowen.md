> [!info] Description
> - Inhabited mostly by Vahadar Elfs
> - Conflict ravaged city, under siege by the army of the army of the [[Nations/Daicon Kingdom/Daicon Kingdom|Daicon Kingdom]] since more than a year
> - Control of the city is hanging on threads, but elven freedom guerilla remains strong
> 

Daicon troops have encircled the city and cut most major roads, hoping to starve the population into surrender. Tarrowen's walls remain intact, but food, medicine, and fresh troops are increasingly scarce. The city has become a rallying point for [[Nations/The Freelands/The Freelands|The Freelands]], though the other cities argue over how much aid they can afford to send.

> [!tldr] NPCs
> - Aelindra Veyr: High Warden of Tarrowen. A pragmatic Vahadar elf commander who refuses to surrender despite increasingly desperate conditions. She has become a symbol of Freeland resistance
> - Cael Thandor: Speaker of the Concord. An elderly high elf diplomat attempting to convince the other cities to commit troops without allowing the Freelands to collapse into a larger war.
> - Lord Eryndor Pellwor: Elven aristocrat from the eastern city of Letharis. Publicly supports Tarrowen, privately believes the siege is an opportunity to weaken his political rivals.
> - Captain Brannic Blyat: Human mercenary commander whose company has been trapped inside Tarrowen since the siege began. His soldiers are among the city's most reliable defenders.
