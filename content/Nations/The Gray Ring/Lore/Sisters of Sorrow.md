> [!info] Description
> The Sisters are an ancient coven that has learned to survive by making themselves useful to whoever holds power. Their specialty is manipulating perception, emotion, memory, and coincidence. Hag covens traditionally become significantly more powerful when working together, including through shared divination and illusion magic, which fits their role as an organized occult asset. MICE officially classified them as “Occult Consultants” in the past.
