> [!info|wide-5] Lord Reginald “Reggie” Cott
> _Human F-tier BBEG_
> - Role: Petty mastermind / bureaucratic supervillain
> - **Alignment:** Lawful Selfish  
> - **Age:** 43  
> - **Occupation:** Regional Assistant Vice-Overseer of MICE
> 
> Reggie is the sort of villain who desperately wants to be feared but is mostly regarded as **an administrative inconvenience**. He wears a black cloak that is slightly too short for him, has a dramatic villainous scar that is actually from falling off a horse, and insists that everyone call him **“Lord Cott”** despite possessing neither a lordship nor a particularly impressive surname. 

![[_assets/Sprites/People/reggieScott.png|465x496]]
### Personality
- Petty beyond reason.
- Extremely passive-aggressive.
- Convinced he's a genius.
- Terrible at improvising.
- Easily offended.
- Obsessed with hierarchy.
- Cowardly when things become dangerous.
- Completely incapable of admitting he doesn't understand something.
- The kind of man who says _“As I have already explained…”_ before explaining something completely incorrectly.
