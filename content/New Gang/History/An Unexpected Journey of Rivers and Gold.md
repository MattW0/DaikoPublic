- The Part can roll a d6 to determine how many nodes they can advance
- After 2- 3 rolls an hostile party will pursue the players by also rolling a d6
	- If the hostile party catches the player an random encounter will trigger
- There are different nodes that will be further explained
	- Nodes you have to end upon to trigger an effect
	- Nodes you only have to pass through to trigger an effect
- To complete the journey the players will have to reach the destination alive

2) Players get the effect of a long rest
3) Players must choose which path to follow
4) Players can purchase Items from an Vendor
5) Players can choose to play a game of Chance
	- DM rolls a d6
	- Players must call high, middle or low
	- Low = 1-2, High = 5-6
	- If they guessed correctly the will gain 1 Healing Potion
	- If they guess incorrectly the will have to pay 25 GP to the mysterious figure
6) Snakes but no ladders
	- If the players land on the river crossing they automatically are swept down the stream
	- If the players only pass through the river crossing they will have to pass a DC12 Saving Throw or be swept down river
